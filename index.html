Questions

Frequently asked questions

Answers to what usually comes up before a first call.

Is this a fit for my brand?

This works best for DTC and e-commerce brands already spending on paid marketing across more than one channel and wanting a clear, unbiased view of what's actually driving revenue. If you're pre-revenue or not yet running paid campaigns, a lighter consulting session is usually a better starting point than a full retainer.

What if my data isn't clean or my tracking is a mess?

That's normal, and it's the starting point of every engagement, not a prerequisite for one. The data audit in step two of the process is exactly where fragmented tracking, inconsistent naming, and missing conversions get sorted out before any dashboard or model is built.

How long until I see dashboards live?

Foundation-tier dashboards are typically live within two to three weeks of kickoff. Full Marketing Mix Modeling builds usually take four to six weeks, depending on how much historical data is available and how many channels are in scope.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. Engagements are month-to-month by default. Committing to 3, 6, or 12 months unlocks a discount on the monthly fee and setup cost, but it's optional, not a requirement to get started.

Who owns the dashboards and models once they're built?

You do. Everything built for your account, dashboards, models, and reporting infrastructure, is yours to keep and use independently, with no vendor lock-in. See the Terms of Service for the full details.

What tools and platforms do you work with?

Looker Studio and Power BI for dashboards, Python and SQL for data work, and Meta Robyn or Google Meridian for Marketing Mix Modeling, plus GeoLift for incrementality testing. The goal is always to fit into your existing stack rather than replace it.
