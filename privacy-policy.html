Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This policy explains what information is collected through sirineanalytics.github.io and how it is used. This is a personal freelance portfolio site, not a large-scale platform, and data collection is kept to a minimum.

Information collected

The site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand which sections visitors read and how they navigate the page. This generates standard analytics data such as approximate location, device type, and time spent on the site. No personal account or profile is created for visitors.

If you book a call through the Cal.com scheduling link or send an email, the information you provide directly (name, email address, and anything you write) is used solely to respond to your inquiry and, if you proceed, to deliver the requested services.

How information is used

  • To respond to inquiries and scheduling requests
  • To improve the site based on aggregate visitor behavior
  • To deliver services once a client engagement begins

Information is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

Booking calls are handled by Cal.com, and analytics are handled by Google Analytics. Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they process data on their platforms.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish visitors and measure site usage. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time, which may affect how analytics data is recorded but will not affect your ability to use the site.

Data retention

Correspondence related to client work is retained for as long as reasonably needed for business and tax purposes. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' default retention settings.

Your rights

You can request to see, correct, or delete any personal information held about you by emailing sirine.bnmnsr@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to sirine.bnmnsr@gmail.com.
