What to Do When Your Marketing Tracking Is a Mess

Short answer

Start with the highest-leverage fixes: consistent UTM parameters, a clean order source of truth, and basic conversion tracking validation. Do not try to fix everything at once.

Priority order of fixes

  1. UTM & campaign naming hygiene — Enforce a simple, documented taxonomy.
  2. Shopify / order data integrity — Ensure source, discount, and customer fields are reliable.
  3. Pixel & Conversion API health — Verify Meta, Google, TikTok events are firing correctly and match order volume directionally.
  4. Refund and cancellation handling — Make sure revenue metrics reflect net numbers.
  5. Cross-domain and consent mode issues (if relevant).
  6. Only then move to advanced attribution or MMM readiness.

Quick audit checklist

  • Do 90%+ of paid orders have clean UTMs?
  • Does platform-reported conversion volume roughly track Shopify order volume?
  • Can you calculate a trustworthy blended CAC from your own data?

Fix the foundation first. Fancy models on dirty data produce expensive confusion.
